# Knowledge Compressor

<ItemImage id="iska_utils:knowledge_compressor" />

## What it does

The **Knowledge Compressor** stores experience as fluid in an internal tank and crafts <ItemImage id="iska_utils:jelly_of_knowledge" /> **Jelly of Knowledge** when enough XP is available.

## How to use

1. Pipe or insert **experience fluid** into the block (same fluid family used elsewhere in Iskandert's Utilities).
2. Each conversion batch turns as much fluid as possible into jellies (up to a full output stack). Default tank capacity is **64000 mB** (one stack of jelly at default costs).
3. Redstone power **pauses** conversion (powered = idle).

## Tips

- Jellies are portable XP: see **Jelly of Knowledge** under **Materials** for how to consume them.
