# Suspicious Delivery

<ItemImage id="iska_utils:suspicious_delivery" />

Artifacts and special curios obtained mainly by opening **Suspicious Delivery**.

## How to obtain

- **Wandering Trader** — **trade** (emeralds) or **defeat** the trader for a chance at a drop (tooltip lines in-game).
- **Optional:** with the **Artifacts** mod, the **Mimic** may rarely drop **Suspicious Delivery** itself — artifacts inside still come only from opening one.

Artifacts such as <ItemImage id="iska_utils:mining_equitizer" /> **Mining Equitizer** or <ItemImage id="iska_utils:necrotic_crystal_heart" /> **Necrotic Crystal Heart** come from opening one, not directly from the Mimic.

## Opening

**Use** it to receive **one random** outcome. The package is **consumed** when opened.

## Luck and Bad Luck

While opening, the vanilla **Luck** effect nudges outcomes toward rarer rewards. **Bad Luck** does the opposite. Stronger effects matter more than a brief sip.

## Possible rewards (examples)

- <ItemImage id="iska_utils:drop_of_entropy" /> **Drop of Entropy**
- delivery artifacts — pages below (including <ItemImage id="iska_utils:arcane_dictionary" /> **Arcane Dictionary**)
- <ItemImage id="iska_utils:durable_shears" /> **Durable Shears**
- Another **Suspicious Delivery**
- Joke outcomes (for example a **clock** after a fake bomb scare) — usually **not** recyclable

## Recycling

You do not have to open every **Suspicious Delivery**.

- **Unopened** **Suspicious Delivery** and most **delivery artifacts** → one <ItemImage id="iska_utils:drop_of_entropy" /> **Drop of Entropy** each (crafting table or **Factory**).
- **Drop of Entropy** cannot be recycled into more drops.

Recyclable delivery items include **Suspicious Delivery** itself, <ItemImage id="iska_utils:ancient_tablet" /> **Ancient Tablet**, <ItemImage id="iska_utils:durable_shears" /> **Durable Shears**, <ItemImage id="iska_utils:arcane_dictionary" /> **Arcane Dictionary**, <ItemImage id="iska_utils:miniature_tent" /> **Miniature Tent**, and the artifacts listed below (Cheese, Totem, Heart, Bone, Gauntlet, Crown, Deception, Ice Diamond, Old Brick, Roots, Equitizer, Candle). Details on the **Drop of Entropy** page under **Entropy materials**.

## Curios

Most artifacts are worn in **Curios** slots. **Arcane Candle** is an exception and works from your hotbar or inventory.

**Entropy materials** (tablet, crystals, entropic gear) are listed in the next hub below.
