# Essence of Knowledge Grate

<ItemImage id="iska_utils:essence_of_knoledge_grate" />

## What it does

A thin grate you place **on top of a fluid tank**. While you stand on it, your experience is drained into **Condensed Knowledge** in the tank below.

## How to use

1. Place it on a machine or fluid tank that accepts Condensed Knowledge — for example a <ItemImage id="iska_utils:knowledge_compressor" /> **Knowledge Compressor** or a <ItemImage id="iska_utils:collecting_crate" /> **Collecting Crate**.
2. The grate needs a solid block underneath; it breaks if that block is removed.
3. Stand on the grate: XP drains at the base rate. Press **Shift** once to toggle acceleration **ON/OFF** (does not need to be held). Acceleration resets to OFF when you step off.

## Tips

- The Knowledge Compressor turns Condensed Knowledge into jelly; the Collecting Crate is better for bulk storage and transfer.
