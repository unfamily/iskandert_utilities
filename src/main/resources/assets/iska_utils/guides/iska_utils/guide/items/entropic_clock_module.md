# Entropic Clock

<ItemImage id="iska_utils:entropic_clock" />

Upgrade item used as a **module** in machines that accept it. Artifact lore and delivery context also live under **Suspicious Delivery** — see [Entropic Clock (artifact)](entropic_clock.md).

## Temporal Overclocker

- **Upgrade slot** (left of the Overclock button): insert an Entropic Clock to raise the **max overclock factor**.
- While installed, accelerating linked blocks consumes **stored entropy** each tick.
- **Fuel slot** (right of the Overclock button): insert **Drop of Entropy** to recharge (percentage shown under the slot).

## Entropised Spawner

- Place in the **Clock** slot to **shorten the delay** between spawn cycles (stacks in the slot).
- Stacks with **entropy fuel** charge, which also speeds the countdown when present.
- Fuel is **not** required to spawn; clocks only affect timing.

## How to obtain

From **Suspicious Delivery**.
