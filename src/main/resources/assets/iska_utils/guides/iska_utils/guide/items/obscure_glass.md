# Obscure Glass

<ItemImage id="iska_utils:obscure_glass" />

## What it does

**Obscure Glass** is a solid, opaque-looking glass block with two key properties:

- **Blocks sky light** — acts as a full opaque block for sky-light propagation, preventing sunlight from passing through even though it is visually transparent.
- **Immune to Wither and Ender Dragon** — cannot be destroyed by Wither skull explosions or Ender Dragon block destruction passes.

Use it to build underground structures, protected vaults, or mob farms that need sky-light blocking without losing the visual glass appearance.
