# Miniature Tent

<ItemImage id="iska_utils:miniature_tent" />

## What it does

Arcane **charm** curio. While equipped, it increases the activation chance of other **Iskandert's Utilities** artifacts (multiplier shown on the item tooltip).

## Obtaining

Often found inside <ItemImage id="iska_utils:suspicious_delivery" /> **Suspicious Delivery** parcels.

## Recycling

Can be recycled into one <ItemImage id="iska_utils:drop_of_entropy" /> **Drop of Entropy** at a crafting table or **Factory**, like other delivery artifacts.
