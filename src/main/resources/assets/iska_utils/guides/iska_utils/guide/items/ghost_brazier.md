# Ghost Brazier

<ItemImage id="iska_utils:ghost_brazier" />

## What it does

- Press the Ghost Brazier keybind to **cast** (default 3s / 60 ticks) into ethereal spectator form.
- Ethereal pool lasts **60s** (1200 ticks) at standard spectator movement; **no drain while still**; moving drains the pool (tracked by position, not walk animation).
- Press again to leave **instantly**. Pool expiry also returns you to physical form.
- On exit you gain **Ethereal Slow Falling** (clears after 3 seconds on the ground).
- PvP hits interrupt an active enter-cast. Teleport is blocked while ethereal.
- Soul / sculk particles and sounds mark cast, ethereal form, and exit.
